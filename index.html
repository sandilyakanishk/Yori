Why Yori?

A Calm Presence for Your
Workspace.

Yori is inspired by the idea of a quiet companion that stays beside you.

Instead of demanding your attention, Yori simply exists with you—listening, reacting, and sharing your workspace.

Whether you're coding, studying, designing, or simply taking a short break, Yori is always present.

Not to replace people.

Not to replace creativity.

Simply to make your workspace feel a little more alive.

Yori isn't another chatbot. It's a companion.
